# Stedding 0.4.0 beta 13

Passkeys with Touch ID on the Mac, split view's last rows, child tabs that fold and close on Back,
a question when a page is open in another window, and more of peek and little windows. Still on
Chromium 157.0.8103.39.

## New

- **Passkeys with Touch ID on the Mac.** A site can save a passkey on your Mac, and Touch ID or your
  password guards it. It stays on that Mac, in that Stedding profile. A passkey in a password
  manager's extension works as before. Passkeys in Apple Passwords need Apple's permission, which
  has been asked for.
- **A page open in another window.** A search in the command bar also finds what is open in your
  other windows, and choosing one asks where to show it: **Show Here** or **Go to Window**. Chromium's
  "Switch to this tab" asks the same.
- **Child tabs, the rest.** With **Show tabs under the tab that opened them** on, Back at a child's
  first page closes it and shows its parent, and ⇧⌘T brings it back. **Fold Child Tabs** on a tab's
  menu or in the command bar folds its children under it, with a count such as +3, and they stay
  open. Close Other Tabs keeps a tab's children. A link that opens its own tab now counts as a child
  too, which it never did.
- **Split view.** A split takes one number for ⌘1–9 and its badge, and the key goes to the pane you
  used last. A tab dropped on a pinned tab's page makes a pinned split, and a tab that joins a pin in
  a split is pinned with it.
- **Peek.** A pin's link that the server redirects to another site opens a peek at the place it was
  sent, and a sign-in stays in the pin. The screenshot keys now take the peek, not the pin under it.
- **Little windows.** ⇧⌘T brings a closed little window back as a little window.

## Fixed

- **Enter in an empty command bar** opens the New Tab page. It went to the tab already on screen.
- **Move Tab in the command bar** moves every selected tab.
- **Turn Bookmarks into Pinned Tabs** is listed only when there is something left to convert.
- **The keys in a private window.** ⌥⌘←/→ step through the tabs and ⌘D adds a bookmark, in a window
  with no Spaces (Ctrl+Alt+←/→ and Ctrl+D on Windows).
- **A route to a deleted Space** no longer stops a link from opening in a little window.
- **The command bar and screen readers.** A screen reader hears it as a dialog with a name.

## Install

- **macOS** (Apple silicon, macOS 13 or later): `Stedding-0.4.0-beta.13-arm64.dmg`, signed and
  notarized. Open it and drag Stedding to Applications.
- **Windows** (10 and 11, x64): `Stedding-0.4.0-beta.13-win-x64.exe`, installs for you alone.
  It is not signed yet: in SmartScreen click **More info**, then **Run anyway**, once.

Installed copies update themselves (Mac from 0.2.0 beta 16, Windows from 0.2.0 beta 19).

## Known limits

- The Windows installer is unsigned (`S-56`).
- Netflix, Disney+, Prime Video and Spotify's web player do not play yet (`S-270`).
- Sync through WebDAV or S3 was checked against local servers, not yet a commercial service. A
  server that only offers Digest sign-in is refused, and S3 uses path-style addresses.
- Arc's splits, archive and pinned folders were built from public descriptions of Arc's files and
  checked on files made to match them, not yet on a real Arc profile.
- Import from Opera was checked on files made to match Opera's, not yet on a real install.
- Dia's sidebar does not come across: Dia keeps it in an encrypted database.
- Making a real passkey with a finger on Touch ID was not tried. A signed build offers Touch ID to
  sites and shows the dialog, and that is as far as the checks went.
- Chromium's tab search still jumps to a page in another window, with no question.
- This build is on Chromium 157.0.8103.39.
- Chromium's built-in list of sites that must load over HTTPS lapses for this build on
  10 January 2027; a release on a newer base comes before then.
