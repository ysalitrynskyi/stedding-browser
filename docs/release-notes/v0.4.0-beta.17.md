# Stedding 0.4.0 beta 17

The bookmarks bar is part of the address row: on a page that colours the row, the bar takes the
same colour, with readable text. On Chromium 157.0.8103.51.

## Fixed

- **The bookmarks bar under a coloured address row.** When the address row took a page's colour,
  the bookmarks bar under it kept a grey of its own, a band between the row and the page, and on
  the Mac its text was dark grey on a dark page's row. The bar now has the row's colour and the
  row's text and icons, in horizontal and vertical tabs and in full screen (GitHub issue 98).
- **The bookmarks bar with the address row hidden.** With the address row hidden, or the address
  in the sidebar, the bookmarks sat low and the page cut them in half. They now sit on the bar's
  middle, and the bar is a strip in the page's colour above it.

## Security

- No change: Chromium 157.0.8103.51, as in beta 16.

## Install

- **macOS** (Apple silicon, macOS 13 or later): `Stedding-0.4.0-beta.17-arm64.dmg`, signed and
  notarized. Open it and drag Stedding to Applications.
- **Windows** (10 and 11, x64): `Stedding-0.4.0-beta.17-win-x64.exe`, installs for you alone.
  It is not signed yet: in SmartScreen click **More info**, then **Run anyway**, once.

Installed copies update themselves (Mac from 0.2.0 beta 16, Windows from 0.2.0 beta 19).

## Known limits

- The Windows installer is unsigned (`S-56`).
- Netflix, Disney+, Prime Video and Spotify's web player do not play yet (`S-270`).
- A Space that shares its session cannot have essentials of its own yet (GitHub issue 87, `S-445`).
- Finished downloads missing from the Downloads list (GitHub issue 90) are not understood yet; the
  steps that lead there would help.
- Sync through WebDAV or S3 was checked against local servers, not yet a commercial service. A
  server that only offers Digest sign-in is refused, and S3 uses path-style addresses.
- Arc's splits, archive and pinned folders were built from public descriptions of Arc's files and
  checked on files made to match them, not yet on a real Arc profile.
- Import from Opera was checked on files made to match Opera's, not yet on a real install.
- Dia's sidebar does not come across: Dia keeps it in an encrypted database.
- Making a real passkey with a finger on Touch ID was not tried.
- Chromium's tab search still jumps to a page in another window, with no question.
- Chromium's built-in list of sites that must load over HTTPS lapses for this build on
  10 January 2027; a release on a newer base comes before then.

## How it was checked

- **The fix:** a browser test and a unit test, each seen failing first, for the bar's colours, its
  place under the row and its buttons with the row hidden. Then the release builds by eye on both
  platforms: light and dark windows over dark, light and orange pages, vertical and horizontal
  tabs, the address row hidden and the address in the sidebar, a private window, and macOS full
  screen.
- **Suites:** on the Mac, 1493 unit tests and 949 browser tests on the release build and on the
  build with DCHECKs and the dangling-pointer detector. All passed on the release build; on the
  DCHECK build one test timed out and passed three times of three alone. On Windows, 1481 unit
  tests on every build, all passing, and 896 browser tests. On the release build all but three
  passed: one fails on the PC's 4K display at 150% at any scale (`S-458`), one passed 5 of 5 alone
  (`S-461`), and a Space switch's slide failed 3 of 5 alone, as it had on beta 16 there (`S-463`).
  On the DCHECK build, at the display's own scale, the tests that read a DIP off at 150% failed as
  on beta 16 (`S-458`), and a drag in a private window passed 10 of 10 alone.
- **AddressSanitizer on Windows:** the unit and browser suites, with no dangling pointer. The same
  150% tests and a frame-timing test failed as on beta 16. Two tests of Settings' search for Arc's
  words crashed when the sanitizer's allocator ran out of memory during the parallel run, and a folder's hover test
  failed; each passed three times of three alone, with no AddressSanitizer report.
- **Acceptance scripts:** the core loop on a copy of the notarized Mac build and on the installed
  Windows copy. On Windows, run right after the installer's cycle with the DCHECK build still
  running beside it, a press on a folder's header left it open and one Ctrl+2 was missed; alone,
  the core loop passed all fourteen steps and the keyboard script all twenty, twice.
- **Updates:** from beta 16 to beta 17 on both platforms through a local feed, the same files
  users get, with the sidebar's width kept on Windows; and on Windows the installer's upgrade,
  uninstall and fresh install.
