# Backlog

**This file is the order of work.** [PLAN.md](PLAN.md) holds the owner's standing
decisions and when an item is done. Ids from the audit of 2026-09-26 (TAB-1, SPC-4, …)
resolve in [docs/history/plan-2026-09-26.md](docs/history/plan-2026-09-26.md); a spec row's
id (B39, H29, …) lives in `docs/features/`.

Other documents cite ids from here and keep no lists of their own. Ids are stable: never
renumber, never reuse. Close an item by moving its row, with the commit or patch that closed
it, to the top of "Closed backlog items" in [docs/history/archive.md](docs/history/archive.md).
`tooling/check-repo` reads ids from both.

Open is in priority order: the owner's next item, then defects, then checks, then
infrastructure, then features. A request in chat comes before this list.
