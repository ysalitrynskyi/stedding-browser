# Stedding 0.6.1

A new window opens quickly however many pins you have, its asleep pins show their sites' icons,
the Space chips at the sidebar's foot sit in the middle again, and a download's start no longer
shows on another Space.

## Fixed

- **A new window with many pins opens quickly.** Since 0.6.0 every window has every pin and
  essential, and a new window made them all in one go: with a few hundred pins the browser
  stood still for several seconds. The window now shows the Space it opens on, with the
  essentials, at once, and brings in the other Spaces' pins one Space at a time while you work.
  Each pin comes in already in its Space and its folder, and the sidebar draws its rows once per
  Space rather than once per tab.
- **Asleep pins show their sites' icons.** A pin asleep in a new window, and a live folder's row,
  showed the generic globe until you chose it. It now shows the icon your history keeps for its
  page, or for its site, until it loads.
- **The Space chips sit in the middle of the sidebar's foot.** With many Spaces the chips stood
  nearer the "+" than the downloads button. They now centre between the two, giving up a little
  of their size, or of the gaps between dots, when the row is full.
- **A download's start stays in its window.** A download clicked in a full-screen window just
  before moving to another Space sent its chip flying, and its button ringing, on the Space you
  moved to. Nothing flies now, and the download toast does not open, while the window is off
  the screen, minimised or on another Space; a window that leaves mid-flight takes the chip
  with it.

## Chromium

- Built on Chromium 157.0.8103.79, as 0.6.0 was. Chromium's 157.0.8103.80 for Mac and Windows
  has the same source; only its version number differs.
- On 2026-11-12 Chromium made M158 stable, with security fixes of its own. Stedding takes a
  Chromium security update within 14 days, so a release on M158 is due by 2026-11-26.

## Install

- **macOS** (Apple silicon, macOS 13 or later): `Stedding-0.6.1-arm64.dmg`, signed and
  notarized. Open it and drag Stedding to Applications.
- **Windows** (10 and 11, x64): `Stedding-0.6.1-win-x64.exe`, installs for you alone. It is not
  signed yet: in SmartScreen click **More info**, then **Run anyway**, once.

Installed copies update themselves (Mac from 0.2.0 beta 16, Windows from 0.2.0 beta 19).

## Known limits

- The Windows installer is unsigned (`S-56`).
- Netflix, Disney+, Prime Video and Spotify's web player do not play yet (`S-270`).
- Spaces that share a session share their essentials; a Space's own essentials with shared
  sign-ins are not offered (GitHub issue 87, `S-445`).
- In a window whose pinned run holds a split, that run keeps its order when another window
  reorders it (`S-94`).
- An exported sidebar file, and the sidebar's backups, hold a live folder's rows as ordinary pins
  (`S-478`).
- A second Space made by sync before 0.6.0 stays until you delete it (`S-494`).
- A peek closed from outside, by its pinned tab closing, the pinned page going to another site,
  the window closing or the browser quitting, does not ask "Leave site?", and what was typed in
  it is lost.
- A pin whose page and site were never opened in Stedding shows the generic globe until chosen;
  the join sheet counts "1 folders" (`S-497`).
- Finished downloads missing from the Downloads list (GitHub issue 90) are not understood yet; the
  steps that lead there would help.
