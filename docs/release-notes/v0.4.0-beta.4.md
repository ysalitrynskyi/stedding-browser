# Stedding 0.4.0 beta 4

Your sidebar comes back whatever On startup says. On Chromium 157.0.8103.31.

## Changed

- **The sidebar comes back at every launch** (GitHub issue 43). Whatever Settings › On startup
  says, the essentials, each Space's pinned tabs and your folders with their tabs come back. The
  choice decides the other tabs only: *Continue where you left off* brings them back too, *Open
  the New Tab page* leaves them closed and adds a New Tab page, and *Open a specific page or set
  of pages* opens those pages. The tabs left closed are under *Archived*. On the Mac a click on
  the Dock with no window open does the same.
- **New Tab pages no longer fill Archived.** A New Tab page has nothing to bring back, so the
  archive leaves it out.

## Install

- **macOS** (Apple silicon, macOS 13 or later): `Stedding-0.4.0-beta.4-arm64.dmg`, signed and
  notarized. Open it and drag Stedding to Applications.
- **Windows** (10 and 11, x64): `Stedding-0.4.0-beta.4-win-x64.exe`, installs for you alone.
  It is not signed yet: in SmartScreen click **More info**, then **Run anyway**, once.

Installed copies update themselves (Mac from 0.2.0 beta 16, Windows from 0.2.0 beta 19).

## Known limits

- The Windows installer is unsigned (`S-56`).
- Netflix, Disney+, Prime Video and Spotify's web player do not play yet (`S-270`).
- On Windows, in a split in a narrow window (about 1040 pixels wide with the sidebar open),
  the address field still runs across both panes when the right pane is the active one: the
  window's own buttons leave it no room over that pane (`S-370`). At 1300 pixels and wider it
  sits over the pane you are in.
- Chromium's built-in list of sites that must load over HTTPS lapses for this build on
  13 December 2026; a release on a newer base comes before then.

## How it was checked

The change has tests that failed before it: the sidebar's return with the New Tab page and with
specific pages, the Dock's, and the archive's New Tab pages. On the final build every unit and
browser test passed on both platforms: on the Mac 1349 unit tests and 685 browser tests, on
Windows 1340 unit tests and 643 browser tests; one Mac frame-timing check paused once and passed
8 of 8 runs again. They passed with the extra checks for dangling pointers and broken
assumptions on both, but for one crash inside Chromium's compositor in a settings test, which
passed 12 of 12 runs again. With the real pointer on the Mac's build, On startup set to the New
Tab page in Settings, a quit and a launch brought back the pin and the folder with its tab, and
left the other tabs under Archived. The acceptance scripts (the core loop, a relaunch, drags,
keys) passed on the Mac's signed build and on Windows' installed copy, and Apple's distribution
check passed. On Windows an installed 0.4.0 beta 3 updated itself to this release through a copy
of the update feed and came back with its tabs, and the installer repaired, uninstalled keeping
the profile, and installed again.
