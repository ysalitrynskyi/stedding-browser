# Stedding 0.5.0

What's playing now shows at the foot of the sidebar, a playing video can pop out when you leave
its tab, Spaces that share a session wear its colour, and the rail makes room for more of your
Spaces.

## New

- **What's playing, at the sidebar's foot.** A tab of the window that plays sound while it is
  not on screen shows a card above Archived: its icon and title, play or pause, and stop. A
  click on the card shows the tab, in its Space. In the collapsed sidebar the card is the tab's
  icon above History, and a click pauses or plays it. ⌥⇧⌘M (Ctrl+Alt+Shift+M) goes to the tab,
  and the command bar can pause, play or stop it. Settings → Stedding → Pages and menus →
  **Show what's playing at the foot of the sidebar**, on by default.
- **Pop out a playing video.** With Settings → Stedding → Pages and menus → **Pop out a playing
  video when you leave its tab** on, a video playing with sound goes into picture in picture
  when you leave its tab or its Space, and back into the page when you show the tab again. Off
  by default.
- **Sessions in colour (GitHub issue 99).** Each named session has a colour. In the sidebar a
  line in that colour runs under the Spaces that share it, and menus, the command bar, Settings
  and the archive show a dot of it. The words are plainer too: "Own session" or "Session:
  Clients" after a Space's name, and a Session submenu on the Space menu.
- **More room in the collapsed sidebar.** With more than four Spaces, the active Space's icon
  leads and the others stand as small dots two to a line under it, where they took a column
  of their own. Each dot still switches to its Space and names it.
- **Bookmark This Tab from the keyboard (GitHub issue 112).** The command bar's actions list
  Bookmark This Tab, and Settings → Stedding → Shortcuts can give it a key. The welcome flow
  can be gone through with the keyboard alone.
- **The archive keeps a little window's link.** A link closed in a little window is a row of
  the archive that says so, with a **Little windows** filter, as Arc keeps Little Arc's links.
- **The command bar finds titles in any script and case**, so a Cyrillic or accented title
  typed in lower case is found. "@Work Stuff" picks the Space named Work Stuff whole.

## Fixed

- **Renamed pins keep their names through a sidebar file (GitHub issue 114).** Names given to
  pinned tabs came back from an imported file only until the page loaded. A file exported by
  an earlier version still holds the name as the page's title; export it again from 0.5.0 to
  keep the names.
- **Separators travel in the sidebar file (GitHub issue 19).** A separator above a row is now
  exported and comes back on import.
- **The sidebar on hover keeps its width (GitHub discussion 117).** Expanded on hover from the
  collapsed sidebar, or slid in from the window's edge when hidden, the sidebar now takes the
  width you gave it, not the default.
- **An extension's button in a peek (GitHub issue 102).** While a peek is open over a pinned
  tab, an extension's button, its popup and its keys act on the peek's page, and its icon and
  badge show that page's state: a password manager counts the peek's logins, not the pinned
  page's.
- **macOS full screen:** the address row's bar starts at the page's card, not 6 points left of
  it.
- **Windows:** ending a tab's rename with Enter or Escape gives the keys back to the page every
  time.
- **Windows:** the sidebar's list scrolls to the tab you switch to in every window. It waited for
  word that the window was on screen, which Windows does not always send.

## Install

- **macOS** (Apple silicon, macOS 13 or later): `Stedding-0.5.0-arm64.dmg`, signed and
  notarized. Open it and drag Stedding to Applications.
- **Windows** (10 and 11, x64): `Stedding-0.5.0-win-x64.exe`, installs for you alone. It is not
  signed yet: in SmartScreen click **More info**, then **Run anyway**, once.

Installed copies update themselves (Mac from 0.2.0 beta 16, Windows from 0.2.0 beta 19).

## Known limits

- The Windows installer is unsigned (`S-56`).
- Netflix, Disney+, Prime Video and Spotify's web player do not play yet (`S-270`).
- Spaces that share a session share their essentials; a Space's own essentials with shared
  sign-ins are not offered (GitHub issue 87, `S-445`).
- Pinned tabs and essentials are per window; a new window does not show them yet (GitHub issue
  111, `S-94`).
- Finished downloads missing from the Downloads list (GitHub issue 90) are not understood yet; the
  steps that lead there would help.
