# 0001 — Product name: Stedding

Status: Accepted
Date: 2026-08-30

## Context

The project needed a permanent name before any public artifact (repo, domain, docs)
existed. The early working name failed vetting: a well-funded software company sells
to the same technical audience under that name — a clear trademark-confusion risk —
and English speakers pronounced it two ways. We generated candidate names from
invented and real English words and coinages across nautical, avian, wayfinding and
shelter themes, and vetted each with live web searches for existing browsers,
software collisions, trademark exposure, pronunciation, and the odds of ranking #1
for the exact phrase "{Name} Browser". Full record: `../NAMING.md`.

## Decision

The product is named **Stedding Browser**. Stedding means a haven: a settled, kept
place, which is what browsing free of surveillance and vendor control should feel
like. The name is shaped on the English word "steading", a farmstead or homestead,
which comes from "stead", an old word for a place. The runner-up, Vesper, was viable
but lost on field cleanliness.

Only the word "Stedding" is used, as this browser's name. No artwork, names,
quotations or other material from any book, film, game or other brand, and no
claimed affiliation with anyone.

## Consequences

- Zero known products, trademarks, or exact-phrase SERP competition for
  "Stedding Browser" at decision time — cheap to defend, easy to rank for.
- The name carries the product thesis; marketing copy can lean on the haven meaning.
- We accept a small familiarity cost: most readers meet the name for the first time.
  It is unusual but easy to say (STED-ding, rhyming with "wedding"), and its shape
  points to the English word "steading".
- Trademark hygiene rules live in `../BRAND.md` and bind all future material.
