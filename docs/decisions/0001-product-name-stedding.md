# 0001 — Product name: Stedding

Status: Accepted
Date: 2026-08-30

## Context

The project needed a permanent name before any public artifact (repo, domain, docs)
existed. The early working name failed vetting: a well-funded cloud-optimization
company with patents sells to the same technical audience under it — a clear
trademark-confusion risk — and the name has a pronunciation split. We generated 38
candidates (short words from invented settings, invented and real English words) and
vetted each with live web
searches for existing browsers, software collisions, trademark exposure,
pronunciation, and the odds of ranking #1 for the exact phrase "{Name} Browser".
Full record: `../NAMING.md`.

## Decision

The product is named **Stedding Browser**. A stedding is a haven — a settled, kept
place — the metaphor for browsing free of surveillance and vendor control. The word
is shaped on "steading" (a homestead), which strengthens the position that we are
using an English-rooted word, not anyone else's asset. The two runner-ups were viable
but lost on meaning and field cleanliness respectively.

We use only the word. No other party's trademarks, artwork, or claimed affiliation,
ever.

## Consequences

- Zero known products, trademarks, or exact-phrase SERP competition for
  "Stedding Browser" at decision time — cheap to defend, easy to rank for.
- The name carries the product thesis; marketing copy can lean on the metaphor.
- We accept a small literacy cost: people who don't know the source read it as an
  unusual but pronounceable English word, which is the fallback we want.
- Trademark hygiene rules live in `../BRAND.md` and bind all future material.
