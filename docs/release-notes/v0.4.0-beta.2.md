# Stedding 0.4.0 beta 2

The fixes from a read of the whole browser, line by line. On Chromium 157.0.8103.31.

## Fixed

- **Spaces with their own session keep their cookies apart.** A link from a shared essential, a
  link opened as a peek, a tab dropped on another window's Space, a reopened tab, a service
  worker's window, and `blob:` and View Source pages could load in another Space's cookies.
- **Closing and quitting.** Closing the window or quitting while a pinned tab asked *Leave site?*
  could leave Stedding unable to quit. Clear in a private window closed the tabs in folders.
  *Clear Independent Session* sent forms again, and could hang.
- **Your profile is safe on first launch.** Two launches at once could delete a profile that had
  just moved into Stedding's folder.
- **Sidebar sync.** Changes that should wait for **Move now** wait, a computer whose clock runs
  far ahead is held back, a re-join keeps the pins of closed windows, a Space's own essential
  moved to the shared row on another computer is shared here, and the newer of two backups
  made in the same second is the one kept.
- **Updates.** On the Mac, a cancelled update no longer installs itself later. On Windows, a
  slow install is no longer reported failed while it is still running.
- **Full-page screenshots** of long or slow pages no longer leave the page changed, and very
  long pages are cut to a size that fits.
- **The command bar.** History and bookmark rows show the address they open, so a title cannot
  pose as another site. Typing an extension's keyword, then a query, hands the query to the
  extension, as the address field does. A search engine that sends its searches as a form keeps
  the query.
- **Links.** Tracking names written with escapes are cleaned too, a search opened in a new tab
  is no longer moved by a "contains" route, a closing peek no longer leaves a second one open,
  and *Copy Link as Markdown* keeps an address with a `)` whole.
- **Restore.** After a crash the right window comes back, a little window comes back little,
  and a failed Blank Window no longer leaves the next window blank.
- **Import.** Arc's folders deeper than two levels come in, folded into the five levels a
  folder can have; Safari, password, history and bookmark imports handle their edge cases.
- **Settings, shortcuts, menus and the browser's pages**, **tabs and drags** (a drag keeps the
  page in full screen), **page colour, site controls and the blocker**: the faults the read
  found, including names that went unescaped into a question.

## Install

- **macOS** (Apple silicon, macOS 13 or later): `Stedding-0.4.0-beta.2-arm64.dmg`, signed and
  notarized. Open it and drag Stedding to Applications.
- **Windows** (10 and 11, x64): `Stedding-0.4.0-beta.2-win-x64.exe`, installs for you alone.
  It is not signed yet: in SmartScreen click **More info**, then **Run anyway**, once.

Installed copies update themselves (Mac from 0.2.0 beta 16, Windows from 0.2.0 beta 19).

## Known limits

- The Windows installer is unsigned (`S-56`).
- Netflix, Disney+, Prime Video and Spotify's web player do not play yet (`S-270`).
- Chromium's built-in list of sites that must load over HTTPS lapses for this build on
  13 December 2026; a release on a newer base comes before then.
