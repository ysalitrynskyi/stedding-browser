# Stedding 0.4.0 beta 10

Chromium 157.0.8103.39, folders that keep the tabs you selected out when they collapse, device
pickers in a Space with its own session, the page's buttons in the site panel when the sidebar
is narrow, and links from other apps as a peek.

## New

- **Collapse a folder and keep working in a few of its tabs.** Select pinned tabs in a folder
  (Ctrl-click or ⌘-click, Shift-click) and collapse it: the selected tabs stay in view while
  they are awake, and the rest fold away (GitHub issue 78). **Keep open tabs out of a collapsed
  folder**, in Settings › Stedding › Tabs and off by default, keeps every awake tab in view
  instead.
- **Links from other apps as a peek.** **Open links from other apps as a peek**, in Settings ›
  Stedding › Peek and little windows and off by default, opens a link from another app as a
  peek over Stedding's window instead of in a window of its own (GitHub issue 74).
- **Back, Forward and Reload in the site panel.** When the sidebar is too narrow for them on
  its top line, the site panel's strip starts with them (GitHub issue 45).

## Fixed

- **Device pickers in a Space with its own session.** Web Serial, WebUSB, WebHID and Web
  Bluetooth pickers, a site's file and folder pickers, and on-device translation and speech
  work in a Space with an independent session, as they do everywhere else. Chromium refused
  them there, and `navigator.serial.requestPort()` failed at once with "No port selected by the
  user" (GitHub issue 73). A site's permissions, devices included, are the profile's in every
  Space; an independent session keeps sign-ins apart.
- **A second window has its window buttons on Windows.** With the address in the sidebar, a
  window opened from another (New Window, Move Tab to New Window, a new blank window) shows
  minimize, maximize and close in its corner on hover (GitHub issue 45).
- **Icons centred in their buttons.** Back, Forward, Reload, the site button, Copy Link and the
  window's buttons sat a point left of their hover circles (GitHub issue 45).
- **A new window on Windows opens clear of the first,** 22 points right and down, as on the Mac.

## Install

- **macOS** (Apple silicon, macOS 13 or later): `Stedding-0.4.0-beta.10-arm64.dmg`, signed and
  notarized. Open it and drag Stedding to Applications.
- **Windows** (10 and 11, x64): `Stedding-0.4.0-beta.10-win-x64.exe`, installs for you alone.
  It is not signed yet: in SmartScreen click **More info**, then **Run anyway**, once.

Installed copies update themselves (Mac from 0.2.0 beta 16, Windows from 0.2.0 beta 19).

## Known limits

- The Windows installer is unsigned (`S-56`).
- Netflix, Disney+, Prime Video and Spotify's web player do not play yet (`S-270`).
- A peek over the page shows no permission prompt of its own; Open as Tab and allow it there.
- Opera is not offered on the import step.
- This build is on Chromium 157.0.8103.39.
- Chromium's built-in list of sites that must load over HTTPS lapses for this build on
  10 January 2027; a release on a newer base comes before then.

## How it was checked

On Windows: an installed 0.4.0 beta 9 with its sidebar set to 300 points found this build in a
signed copy of the update feed, installed it, restarted into it and kept the width; the installer
upgraded beta 9, uninstalled keeping the profile and installed again; and the acceptance scripts
passed on the installed copy, the core loop, the drags and the keyboard, 39 steps. The build with
DCHECKs and the dangling-pointer detector caught a link from another app, opened as a peek,
marked as a page's navigation; that was fixed and has its own test. This build then ran every
suite with DCHECKs and on release (1375 unit tests, 885 browser tests). Each run had one failure
among its neighbours, the place of a toast under a bar and the device picker; each of those
passed five runs of five alone, and they now run one at a time. Every suite passed under
AddressSanitizer with no report.

On the Mac: the signed and notarized build passed Apple's distribution check, and a test copy of
it ran the acceptance scripts. A clipboard manager held ⇧⌘C, so Copy Link's toast was not seen
there and the split after it did not open; the split then opened alone. The unit tests (1384)
and the 924 browser tests passed on the release build and on the build with DCHECKs and the
dangling-pointer detector.

The device pickers were checked with a stand-in serial port, not a real board.
