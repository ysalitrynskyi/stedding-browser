# Stedding 0.4.0 beta 15

Sync no longer shows every pinned tab twice after a second computer joins, and a peek fades in
when it opens. Still on Chromium 157.0.8103.39.

## New

- **A peek fades in.** A peek over the page comes in over a fifth of a second when it opens, so
  the card is there while the page draws. It follows **Animate the sidebar and overlays** in
  Settings › Stedding and your system's Reduce Motion: with either, the peek shows at once, as
  before (GitHub issue 91).

## Fixed

- **Sync showed every pinned tab twice.** When a second computer joined with **Combine**, a Space
  that was never given a name (a fresh install's first Space, shown as "Space 1") was not paired
  with the other computer's, so each computer's pins arrived on the other as a second copy. Such a
  Space now pairs by the name the sidebar shows. Copies made before this version stay: delete the
  extra Space on one computer, and the other asks before it applies the deletion (GitHub issue 89).

## Install

- **macOS** (Apple silicon, macOS 13 or later): `Stedding-0.4.0-beta.15-arm64.dmg`, signed and
  notarized. Open it and drag Stedding to Applications.
- **Windows** (10 and 11, x64): `Stedding-0.4.0-beta.15-win-x64.exe`, installs for you alone.
  It is not signed yet: in SmartScreen click **More info**, then **Run anyway**, once.

Installed copies update themselves (Mac from 0.2.0 beta 16, Windows from 0.2.0 beta 19).

## Known limits

- The Windows installer is unsigned (`S-56`).
- Netflix, Disney+, Prime Video and Spotify's web player do not play yet (`S-270`).
- A Space that shares its session cannot have essentials of its own yet (GitHub issue 87, `S-445`).
- Finished downloads missing from the Downloads list (GitHub issue 90) are not understood yet; the
  steps that lead there would help.
- Sync through WebDAV or S3 was checked against local servers, not yet a commercial service. A
  server that only offers Digest sign-in is refused, and S3 uses path-style addresses.
- Arc's splits, archive and pinned folders were built from public descriptions of Arc's files and
  checked on files made to match them, not yet on a real Arc profile.
- Import from Opera was checked on files made to match Opera's, not yet on a real install.
- Dia's sidebar does not come across: Dia keeps it in an encrypted database.
- Making a real passkey with a finger on Touch ID was not tried.
- Chromium's tab search still jumps to a page in another window, with no question.
- This build is on Chromium 157.0.8103.39.
- Chromium's built-in list of sites that must load over HTTPS lapses for this build on
  10 January 2027; a release on a newer base comes before then.

## How it was checked

- **Suites:** on the Mac, 1492 unit tests and 915 browser tests on the release build and on the
  build with DCHECKs and the dangling-pointer detector. On Windows, the same two suites on both
  builds. All passed. On the Mac's DCHECK build one restore test and one Space session test timed
  out once among the others, and each passed alone.
- **AddressSanitizer on Windows:** the unit and browser suites, with no AddressSanitizer report and no
  dangling pointer.
- **The fixes:** two profiles syncing through WebDAV and through S3 take the steps of issue 89 in a
  test, which showed every pin twice before the fix. The peek's fade was recorded on the signed Mac
  build, and its test fails without it.
- **Acceptance scripts:** the core loop on an installed Windows copy and on a copy of the notarized
  Mac build, all passing. One part on Windows failed right after the installer cycle and passed 14
  of 14 alone, as it has before.
- **Updates:** from beta 14 to beta 15 on both platforms through a local feed, the same files users
  get, with the sidebar's width kept on Windows.
