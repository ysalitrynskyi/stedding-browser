# 0001 — Product name: Stedding

Status: Accepted
Date: 2026-08-30

## Context

The project needed a permanent name before any public artifact (repo, domain, docs)
existed. The working name "Sedai" failed vetting: Sedai Inc (sedai.io) is a
well-funded cloud-optimization company with patents selling to the same technical
audience — a clear trademark-confusion risk — and the name has a pronunciation split
(seh-DAY vs seh-DYE). We generated 38 candidates (Wheel of Time deep cuts, Sanderson
Cosmere terms, invented and real English words) and vetted each with live web
searches for existing browsers, software collisions, trademark exposure,
pronunciation, and the odds of ranking #1 for the exact phrase "{Name} Browser".
Full record: `../NAMING.md`.

## Decision

The product is named **Stedding Browser**. A stedding is a haven in Robert Jordan's
Wheel of Time where the One Power cannot touch you — the metaphor for browsing free
of surveillance and vendor control. The word is also a real English dialect variant
of "steading" (a homestead), which strengthens the position that we are using an
English word, not a Wheel of Time asset. Runner-ups Hawkwing and Vesper were viable
but lost on meaning and field cleanliness respectively.

We use only the word. No Wheel of Time trademarks, artwork, or claimed affiliation,
ever.

## Consequences

- Zero known products, trademarks, or exact-phrase SERP competition for
  "Stedding Browser" at decision time — cheap to defend, easy to rank for.
- The name carries the product thesis; marketing copy can lean on the metaphor.
- We accept a small literacy cost: people who don't know the source read it as an
  unusual but pronounceable English word, which is the fallback we want.
- Trademark hygiene rules live in `../BRAND.md` and bind all future material.
