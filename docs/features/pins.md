# Feature: Pinned tabs

Status: **H1–H6 and H8–H29 built. H7 partial** (Peek reads the stored home; no browser test drifts a pin and follows its link).
Owner docs: `docs/PRODUCT.md` §1, `docs/features/spaces.md` (the two tiers).

A pin remembers its home page. ⌘W puts it to sleep instead of closing it. While "Mark pinned tabs that left their page" is on, a drifted pin shows a mark and can be sent home from its favicon.
