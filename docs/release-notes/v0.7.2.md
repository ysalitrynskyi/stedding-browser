# Stedding 0.7.2

A security update: Stedding is now on Chromium 158.0.8127.89, with Google's fix for a critical
flaw. On the Mac, a two-finger swipe across the sidebar now shows the next Space beside the one
you are in.

## Security

- **Chromium 158.0.8127.89.** Chrome's stable update of 23 November fixes CVE-2026-134401,
  rated critical, in Variations, the part of Chromium that turns features on and off by field
  trial.

## Changed

- **On the Mac, a swipe shows the next Space.** While two fingers swipe across the sidebar, the
  Space they bring in is drawn beside the one you are in, with its name and its rows, and the two
  move together like one page dragged across. Let go past a short distance and it comes in; let
  go sooner and both slide back. Before, only your Space moved, fading, and the next one appeared
  only once you let go.
- The New Tab page's most-visited tiles show their sites' icons at the screen's resolution, as
  Chrome does.

## Install

- **macOS** (Apple silicon, macOS 13 or later): `Stedding-0.7.2-arm64.dmg`, signed and
  notarized. Open it and drag Stedding to Applications.
- **Windows** (10 and 11, x64): `Stedding-0.7.2-win-x64.exe`, installs for you alone. It is not
  signed yet: in SmartScreen click **More info**, then **Run anyway**, once.

Installed copies update themselves (Mac from 0.2.0 beta 16, Windows from 0.2.0 beta 19).

## Known limits

- The Windows installer is unsigned (`S-56`).
- Netflix, Disney+, Prime Video and Spotify's web player do not play yet (`S-270`).
- Spaces that share a session share their essentials; a Space's own essentials with shared
  sign-ins are not offered (GitHub issue 87, `S-445`).
- In a window whose pinned run holds a split, that run keeps its order when another window
  reorders it (`S-94`).
- A second Space made by sync before 0.6.0 stays until you delete it (`S-494`).
- A peek closed from outside, by its pinned tab closing, the pinned page going to another site,
  the window closing or the browser quitting, does not ask "Leave site?", and what was typed in
  it is lost.
- A pin whose page and site were never opened in Stedding shows the generic globe until chosen.
- On the Mac, in a right-to-left language with the sidebar and the address row hidden, a band
  stays above the page (`S-558`).
