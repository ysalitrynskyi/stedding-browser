# Stedding 0.4.0 beta 7

A frosted edge round a peek, privacy choices in the welcome flow, the address in the sidebar laid
out as GitHub issue 45's version B, page bars under the page, and folders that take an icon. On
Chromium 157.0.8103.31.

## Changed

- **A peek has an edge.** A peek over the page now has a frosted rim round its card, so a dark
  peek stands out on a dark page and a light one on a light page. Settings › Stedding › Peek and
  little windows › **Edge of a peek**: Frosted rim (the default), Soft glow or None.
- **Privacy choices in the welcome flow.** On a new installation, a step after the one about
  behaviour says what Stedding already does (third-party cookies blocked, Chromium's own
  advertising features off) and offers three switches: **Ask sites not to sell or share your
  data** (Global Privacy Control, on), **Block ads and trackers** (off; how it works, and that
  an extension can block more) and **Remove tracking from links you open** (off). The same
  switches are in Settings › Stedding › Privacy.
- **Removing tracking from links is off by default.** It can strip the code a site credits a
  referral by. If you turned it on yourself it stays on; if you never touched it, it is now off,
  and Settings › Stedding › Privacy › **Remove tracking from links you open** turns it on.
- **The address field is wide and centred by default.** If you never chose a width, the field
  now sits centred on the page, 800 points wide, and the empty row on either side moves the
  window. Settings › Stedding › **Address field** › Full width brings back the whole row.
- **The address in the sidebar, version B** (GitHub issue 45). With **Show the address in the
  sidebar** on, the sidebar's top line has its toggle at the start and Back, Forward, Reload
  and the menu together at the end, all one size. The field has the site button at its start,
  which opens the site panel, then the site, then Install App, Copy Link and your extensions.
  With ad and tracker blocking on, the site panel has a switch for blocking on that site. On
  the Mac the traffic lights show only while the pointer is over the sidebar's top, as
  Windows' own buttons do.
- **Page bars sit under the page.** "Stedding isn't your default browser", the note to reload a
  page after you change its site's settings, and the browser's other bars are no longer a grey
  band across the top of the page. They come as a bar in the window's colour under the page,
  rising a little over its bottom edge so you see it, and the page gets its height back when
  the bar goes.
- **The Undo after closing tabs** sits on the page's middle, under the address row, as a card
  with a plain Undo. Copy Link's note and a screenshot's sit there too.
- **Folders** (GitHub issue 68). A folder's name lines up with the titles of the tabs beside
  it, and a folder can take an icon: right-click its header, choose **Change Icon…** and type
  or paste a character, or pick an emoji. **Remove Icon** brings the folder glyph back.
- **A tab opened in the background is the next one ⌃⇥ reaches** (GitHub issue 71), as in Arc:
  ⌘-click a link, or choose Open Link in New Tab, and a ⌃⇥ tap shows that tab. Settings ›
  Stedding › Tabs › **⌃⇥ goes to a tab opened in the background first** turns it off.
- **Closing the sidebar to nothing** slides its rows out whole, and back in whole when it
  opens, instead of squeezing them on the way.
- **Full screen on the Mac** follows the address row's rules: hidden with the sidebar, the row
  stays hidden, ⇧⌘D works there, no empty band is left at the top, and the strip above the row
  is the window's colour, not grey.
- **Settings.** Each Space in the Spaces card takes two lines, so its switches and sites no
  longer run out of the card. "Mark pinned tabs that left their page" is now **Show when a
  pinned tab leaves its page**, with a line saying what it marks; the slash before a title is
  part of it, so with it off, as it is by default, no row shows one. The Privacy card says in
  plain words which of Chromium's advertising features are off.

## New settings

- **Edge of a peek**: Frosted rim, Soft glow or None.
- **Change Icon…** and **Remove Icon** on a folder's menu.
- **⌃⇥ goes to a tab opened in the background first**, on by default.
- The welcome flow's privacy step, above.

## Fixed

- Putting pinned tabs to sleep one by one no longer wakes the one slept before it (GitHub issue
  72). The tab shown next is one that is awake; with none awake, a new tab opens in the Space.
- The chip at the start of the address field, such as Stedding's on a settings page, centres
  its icon on its label.
- In a sidebar expanded from the rail on hover, a scroll no longer puts the toggle back on the
  traffic lights' row.

## Install

- **macOS** (Apple silicon, macOS 13 or later): `Stedding-0.4.0-beta.7-arm64.dmg`, signed and
  notarized. Open it and drag Stedding to Applications.
- **Windows** (10 and 11, x64): `Stedding-0.4.0-beta.7-win-x64.exe`, installs for you alone.
  It is not signed yet: in SmartScreen click **More info**, then **Run anyway**, once.

Installed copies update themselves (Mac from 0.2.0 beta 16, Windows from 0.2.0 beta 19).

## Known limits

- The Windows installer is unsigned (`S-56`).
- Netflix, Disney+, Prime Video and Spotify's web player do not play yet (`S-270`).
- A peek over the page shows no permission prompt of its own; Open as Tab and allow it there.
- A folder's icon is not yet in the sidebar file or in sidebar sync (`S-393`).
- Opera is not offered on the import step.
- Chromium's built-in list of sites that must load over HTTPS lapses for this build on
  13 December 2026; a release on a newer base comes before then.

## How it was checked

On Windows, the release build: an installed 0.4.0 beta 6 with its sidebar set to 300 points found
beta 7 in a copy of the update feed, installed it, restarted into it and kept the width; the
installer then repaired over it, uninstalled keeping the profile and installed again. Every suite
passed on the release build, on the build with DCHECKs and the dangling-pointer detector, and under
AddressSanitizer, which found no memory errors (1362 unit tests, 858 browser tests), apart from two
new tests that were themselves wrong and pass after their fix, which changed no browser code. The
acceptance scripts passed on the installed copy: the core loop, the drags and the keyboard, 39
steps. On the Mac, the signed and notarized build passed its acceptance run and every suite on the
release build and on the build with DCHECKs (1371 unit tests, 886 browser tests), with the same
test fixed; Apple's distribution check passed. Issues 71 and 72 were tried with the real pointer
and keyboard on the Mac build. Before the release four reviewers read every change since beta 6;
what they found that could reach a user was fixed, each with a test, and the rest is listed for
the next release.
