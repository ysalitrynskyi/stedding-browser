# Stedding 0.4.0 beta 5

Site icons in the command bar, and new sidebar and appearance settings. On Chromium 157.0.8103.31.

## Changed

- **Site icons in the command bar** (GitHub issue 58). Every row of ⌘T's bar (Ctrl+T on
  Windows) starts with an icon: an open tab shows its own, and a closed tab, a page from your
  history or bookmarks and a site you type show that site's icon. A search shows a magnifier.
  What a row says besides its title, such as *Switch to Tab*, the Space or *History*, now sits at
  the row's right end, smaller, so the titles line up and read first. Commands (⇧⌘P) keep their
  plain rows, with their shortcuts at the right.

## New settings

All in Settings › Stedding, and in the command bar's actions (⇧⌘P, Ctrl+Shift+P on Windows)
(GitHub issue 45).

- **Show a hidden sidebar.** With the sidebar set to hide completely, how long the pointer
  rests at the window's edge before it slides in: at once, or after 0.05, 0.1 (as before),
  0.25 or 0.5 seconds. The right edge waits 0.2 seconds more, for the page's scroll bar.
- **Sidebar animation.** Normal, Fast or Instant, for the sidebar opening, closing, sliding
  in and changing Space.
- **Hover delay** gains 0.25 and 0.5 seconds.
- **Show the address in the sidebar.** Back, Forward and Reload at the top of the sidebar,
  next to the window's buttons, and the page's site in a field under them, as in Arc. Click
  the field to edit the address. The address row hides while the sidebar is open; on Windows
  it stays, for the window's own buttons. Off by default.
- **Essentials per row.** Automatic (as before), up to 3, or up to 4.
- **Appearance**, a new card. **Window colour**: Stedding's own, a neutral grey, or a colour
  you pick for the light look and one for the dark. **Space colour strength**: how much of
  the Space's colour the window takes, from none to strong. **Tab highlight**: how strongly
  the tab you are on stands out, subtle, normal or strong.

## Install

- **macOS** (Apple silicon, macOS 13 or later): `Stedding-0.4.0-beta.5-arm64.dmg`, signed and
  notarized. Open it and drag Stedding to Applications.
- **Windows** (10 and 11, x64): `Stedding-0.4.0-beta.5-win-x64.exe`, installs for you alone.
  It is not signed yet: in SmartScreen click **More info**, then **Run anyway**, once.

Installed copies update themselves (Mac from 0.2.0 beta 16, Windows from 0.2.0 beta 19).

## Known limits

- The Windows installer is unsigned (`S-56`).
- Netflix, Disney+, Prime Video and Spotify's web player do not play yet (`S-270`).
- Chromium's built-in list of sites that must load over HTTPS lapses for this build on
  13 December 2026; a release on a newer base comes before then.

## How it was checked

Each change has tests of its own: the command bar's icons and the detail at a row's end, each
new setting's row in Settings and in the command bar, the edge's rest and the sidebar's speed,
the address in the sidebar and when the address row hides, the essentials grid's rows, and each
window colour, tint and highlight. On the final build every unit and browser test passed on both
platforms: on the Mac 1355 unit tests and 693 browser tests, on Windows 1346 unit tests and 651
browser tests. They passed with the extra checks for dangling pointers and broken assumptions on
Windows. On the Mac, two passed alone but not once in that run: a frame-timing check, whose
bound now allows for that build being twice as slow (6 of 6 runs), and a pinned-tab test that
timed out once (3 of 3 runs). The first final run had found a crash in a test of the sidebar's
top, fixed before these builds. With the real keyboard and pointer on the Mac's build: the
command bar's icons in the dark and light looks, the address in the sidebar, the rail, and four
window looks. On Windows' installed build a capture showed the address in the sidebar with Neutral
grey and a Strong highlight. The acceptance scripts (the core loop, a relaunch, drags, keys)
passed on the Mac's signed build and on Windows' installed copy; the five Mac captures that
differ from beta 4's are the command bar's, now with icons, each read against its step. Apple's
distribution check passed. On Windows an installed 0.4.0 beta 4 updated itself to this release
through a copy of the update feed and came back with its tabs, and the installer repaired,
uninstalled keeping the profile, and installed again.
