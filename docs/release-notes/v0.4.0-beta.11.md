# Stedding 0.4.0 beta 11

Chromium 157.0.8103.39, folders that move between Spaces, Arc's pinned folders, splits and
archive on import, import from Opera, permission prompts in a peek, and a notice on sites that
need DRM.

## New

- **Move a folder to another Space.** **Move to Space** on a folder's menu, or drag its header
  onto another Space's icon. The folder goes whole, nested folders included, and its tabs keep
  their order and their pins. A folder of pins lands at the end of that Space's pinned tabs.
- **More of Arc comes across.** Pinned folders arrive as pinned folders, splits as splits and
  archived items in the archive, and a name you gave a tab in Arc stays after its page loads
  (GitHub issue 80). Import from Arc in the bar shows what will come before anything does.
- **Where things went after an Arc import.** A page says where each thing from Arc is here, and
  Arc's words find the right settings when you search for them.
- **Import from Opera,** on the import step, on both platforms. Speed Dial, the trash and
  pinboards do not come. Opera GX is not offered.
- **One list of routes.** **Sites routed to Spaces**, in Settings › Stedding, lists every site
  that opens in a Space, and **Route sites here…** on a Space's menu goes straight to it.
- **Permission prompts in a peek.** A page in a peek over the page asks for the camera, the
  microphone or your location over the peek. Open as Tab keeps the question.
- **A notice on sites that need DRM.** Netflix, Disney+, Prime Video and Spotify's web player do
  not play yet. These sites now say so, with a button to open the page in another browser. The
  page itself is not blocked.
- **A folder's − on hover.** A folder in the pinned tabs shows a − while the pointer is on its
  header and it holds an open tab. It closes the open tabs and collapses the folder (GitHub
  issue 78). Close Open Tabs and Collapse on its menu does the same.

## Fixed

- **A crash when a tab with a dialog came to the front** (GitHub issue 82).
- **Space in a rename.** Renaming a pinned tab that is not the open one, or a folder, the first
  Space closed the field (GitHub issue 81).
- **An extension's site settings** opened an empty page from `chrome://extensions` in a Space
  with its own session (GitHub issue 79).
- **A password manager's second window in macOS full screen.** A popup opened with another popup
  in front of a full-screen window stays over it. It took a full-screen space of its own, and the
  Mac slid away from the page.
- **Tabs given on the command line** sometimes never loaded, on https addresses too. Updates to the
  certificate store that change nothing no longer drop their connections.
- **A pin's place.** A pin, or a tab just unpinned, no longer lands on the wrong side of another
  Space's tab among the pinned tabs.
- **An update download that stalls** tries again, and About says how to retry.
- **A deleted Space's routes** go with it, five more ad-click ids are cleaned from links, and a
  double click that started off the sidebar's edge no longer resets its width.
- **A selected folder and the selected tab under it** stand apart, and the circle lit under the
  shield and the moon is smaller and dimmer.
- **Arc imports** count what they dropped on the result line, say when Arc is still running, and
  bring pins and tabs with Spaces unticked.

## Install

- **macOS** (Apple silicon, macOS 13 or later): `Stedding-0.4.0-beta.11-arm64.dmg`, signed and
  notarized. Open it and drag Stedding to Applications.
- **Windows** (10 and 11, x64): `Stedding-0.4.0-beta.11-win-x64.exe`, installs for you alone.
  It is not signed yet: in SmartScreen click **More info**, then **Run anyway**, once.

Installed copies update themselves (Mac from 0.2.0 beta 16, Windows from 0.2.0 beta 19).

## Known limits

- The Windows installer is unsigned (`S-56`).
- Netflix, Disney+, Prime Video and Spotify's web player do not play yet (`S-270`).
- Arc's splits, archive and pinned folders were built from public descriptions of Arc's files and
  checked on files made to match them, not yet on a real Arc profile.
- Import from Opera was checked on files made to match Opera's, not yet on a real install.
- Dia's sidebar does not come across: Dia keeps it in an encrypted database.
- A `<permission>` element on a page in a peek shows no prompt; Open as Tab and allow it there.
- This build is on Chromium 157.0.8103.39.
- Chromium's built-in list of sites that must load over HTTPS lapses for this build on
  10 January 2027; a release on a newer base comes before then.

## How it was checked

On Windows: an installed 0.4.0 beta 10 with its sidebar set to 300 points found this build in a
signed copy of the update feed, installed it, restarted into it and kept the width; the installer
upgraded beta 10, uninstalled keeping the profile and installed again; and the acceptance scripts
passed on the installed copy, the core loop, the drags and the keyboard, 39 steps. On the installed
copy, a download that went quiet at 30 MB started over 70 seconds later and finished. Every suite
passed on the release build (1432 unit tests, 978 browser tests). A build with DCHECKs and the
dangling-pointer detector passed them too; it ran beside other builds, and the tests that ran out of
time there passed alone with a longer limit. One test of the list's scrolling, which has failed on
its premise before, failed once in a run of the release build and in one of six runs alone (`S-291`).

On the Mac: the signed and notarized build passed Apple's distribution check, and a test copy of it
ran the acceptance scripts. A copy of the published beta 10 found an earlier build of this release in
a signed local feed and installed it; on this release, a download that went quiet at 30 MB started
over 71 seconds later and finished. Every suite passed on the release build and on the build with
DCHECKs and the dangling-pointer detector (1444 unit tests, 1015 browser tests). On release, one test
that timed out passed alone.

Each change was looked at on a real build, light and dark. Moving a folder was done with the real
pointer on both platforms, with a pinned folder, a nested one and a Space with its own session.

The stand-in for a password manager is an extension making the same call in real macOS full screen,
not Bitwarden itself. Arc's pinned folders, splits and archive, and the import from Opera, were
checked on files made to match theirs, not on real profiles. A move of a folder between two
computers through sidebar sync has not been tried.

The run under AddressSanitizer had not finished when this release was published.
