# Feature: Routing sites to Spaces

Status: **D1–D4, D7, D8, D10 and D11 built. D5 partial** (the Space menu's second pass, BACKLOG S-126). **D6 partial** (no test pairs a peek with a route). **D9 withdrawn.**
Owner docs: `docs/PRODUCT.md` §2. Patch: 0027.

A site opens in the Space it belongs to, wherever the link came from. Stedding keeps a small list of rules in the profile — a site, a substring, an exact address, a host or a host suffix, and the Space it goes to — plus one default Space for links from other apps. A tab that opens with a matching address moves to its Space at once. With "Show notices about Spaces" on (off by default), a toast says where it went and undoes it.

## Notes

- Routes name a Space by its registry id, so they apply in every normal window that has that Space. A Blank Window's Spaces are not in the profile registry, so a route does not move a tab there. The window that receives an external link is the last active one.
- The insert hook runs inside the tab strip's notification, so a route changes membership at once, and the Space switch and the toast are posted a turn later (spaces B4). The importer and sidebar sync hold a `ScopedRoutingPause`: their tabs already know their Space.
- A fresh tab is one whose last committed address is empty, `about:blank`, or a new-tab page. A typed or generated omnibox navigation in it is routed. A link click or a redirect is not. A settings page is not fresh.
- The external-open mark lasts ten seconds and is spent by the first insert of that address. A link opened twice from another app routes twice only if the system hands it over twice.
