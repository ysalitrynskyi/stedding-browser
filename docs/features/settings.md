# Feature: Settings surface

Status: **T1–T24 and T26–T36 built. T25 withdrawn.** VoiceOver on the Space bins has not been heard (S-148).
Owner docs: `docs/PRODUCT.md` (settings).

Stedding's settings are one section of chrome://settings, first in the menu, titled Stedding. Each visible row is one preference: Stedding's own, in `chrome/browser/ui/stedding/stedding_prefs.h`, or a Chromium preference the row leans on. Most are the profile's. The sidebar's density and text size, Global Privacy Control, ad and tracker blocking, Updates and the user's shortcuts are the browser's (local state), shared by every profile. A feature that gains a preference adds a row here and a behaviour in its own spec.
