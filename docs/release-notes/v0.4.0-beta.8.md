# Stedding 0.4.0 beta 8

Tidy names for pinned tabs and downloads, a page of its own for a Space with nothing awake, a
bigger little window, a download you can see start, important updates said plainly, and on the Mac
an optional row beside the camera in full screen. On Chromium 157.0.8103.31.

## New

- **Tidy names, on your device.** Two switches, both off at first. Settings › Stedding › Tabs ›
  **Tidy the titles of pinned tabs** drops the site's name, unread counts and your address from
  a pinned tab's title: "Inbox (3) – you@x.com – Gmail" reads "Inbox". Settings › Stedding ›
  Pages and menus › **Tidy the names of downloaded files** saves a file named like
  "download (3).pdf", or a long code, under the title of the page it came from. Rules on the
  device do it; nothing leaves the machine. A name you give a tab, or type when saving, always
  wins.
- **A Space with nothing awake shows its own page.** When putting tabs to sleep leaves nothing
  awake in a Space, the window no longer opens a New Tab there each time. It shows a quiet page
  in the Space's colour instead: the Space's name, the key for the command bar, a tip at a time,
  and one line about supporting the project. Click a tab in the sidebar, or press ⌘T (Ctrl+T),
  and it goes. Nothing on it is fetched from the internet.
- **The little window is bigger.** A link from another app opens over your window at most of
  its size, as before but larger. Resize one, and the next opens at your size.
- **You can see a download start.** With the sidebar, a chip now flies from the page to the
  sidebar's downloads button and the button rings once. With Settings › Stedding › Pages and
  menus › **Animate the sidebar and overlays** off, the button rings without the flight.
- **Important updates say so.** When an update fixes something serious, the sidebar's update
  pill turns red and reads **Important Update**, and a bar under the page says why, once each
  time Stedding starts, with **Restart Now**. Nothing restarts by itself.
- **The row beside the camera** (Mac, MacBooks with a camera housing). Settings › Stedding ›
  Pages and menus › **Use the row beside the camera in full screen**, off at first, makes full screen
  Stedding's own: Back, Forward and Reload left of the camera and the address right of it, with
  the menu bar and Dock hidden until the pointer reaches them. A video's own full screen stays
  macOS's.

## Changed

- **The address in the sidebar** (GitHub issue 45). On Windows the window's own buttons are in
  the window's top-right corner and show when the pointer is there, so the sidebar's top line
  keeps its room for Back, Forward, Reload and the menu. On the Mac the traffic lights always
  show. **Install App** is a row in the site panel rather than a button in the field. The
  extensions pinned to the field fold into their menu before they push the site's name out, and
  stay on every page.
- **Page bars in popups and app windows** are Chromium's band again, not the bar under the page.
- The **Undo after closing tabs**, and Copy Link's note, stay on the middle of their own page in
  a split.

## Fixed

- Starting Stedding with `--incognito` no longer crashes.
- On the Mac, the welcome flow no longer looks at Safari's data, which made macOS say Stedding
  tried to access data from other apps. Safari's bookmarks come in from a file Safari exports.
- On a sign-in step, the site panel's blocking switch shows the site's own setting, and turning
  it on no longer turns blocking off for that site.
- In the sidebar's address field, turning Copy Link off gives its room back to the site's name
  at once.
- A folder's icon now travels in the sidebar file and in sidebar sync.
- On a narrow sidebar on Windows, the window's menu keeps a place on the top line.

## New settings

- **Tidy the titles of pinned tabs** and **Tidy the names of downloaded files**, both off.
- **Use the row beside the camera in full screen** (Mac), off.

## Install

- **macOS** (Apple silicon, macOS 13 or later): `Stedding-0.4.0-beta.8-arm64.dmg`, signed and
  notarized. Open it and drag Stedding to Applications.
- **Windows** (10 and 11, x64): `Stedding-0.4.0-beta.8-win-x64.exe`, installs for you alone.
  It is not signed yet: in SmartScreen click **More info**, then **Run anyway**, once.

Installed copies update themselves (Mac from 0.2.0 beta 16, Windows from 0.2.0 beta 19).

## Known limits

- The Windows installer is unsigned (`S-56`).
- Netflix, Disney+, Prime Video and Spotify's web player do not play yet (`S-270`).
- A peek over the page shows no permission prompt of its own; Open as Tab and allow it there.
- Opera is not offered on the import step.
- This build is on Chromium 157.0.8103.31; Chromium's newer 157 point release comes in the next
  release.
- Chromium's built-in list of sites that must load over HTTPS lapses for this build on
  13 December 2026; a release on a newer base comes before then.
